## The premise

The *Game of Thrones* opening titles, restaged in Vaslui County. The camera flies over a map of the
county. At each "land" a village rises out of the map like clockwork, and an army marches out of it.
There are five lands and five armies. They all converge on the village square, where a little king sits on
the Vaslui Iron Throne. Every army leader bows. The king grins. Title: **VASLUI OF THRONES**.

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, about 60 s, no dialogue. It works the way episode 3 worked: a famous opening, beat for beat.
- **Look:** the series' locked 3D game style (episodes 1, 2 and 4), not the flat 2D Simpsons look of episode 3.
- **Sound:** the client wants the GoT theme redone as **manele**. The renders carry diegetic SFX only (project rule:
  no music in any render). The manele theme gets laid in the edit, and where it comes from is open question 2.

## The map (the spine of the episode)

The GoT opening's parchment map, rebuilt as **Vaslui County**. It is drawn on an old tablecloth or a
feed sack, with mud stains and a ring left by a tuica glass. In the sky above it sits the astrolabe, a burning sun ringed by
brass bands, made Vaslui style: barrel hoops with a glowing **onion** at the centre. Each
land's name is lettered on the map in the GoT serif and read by the camera as it flies over.
Between lands the camera travels high over the map, the same move as the original.

## New assets this needs

**Characters**
- **The little king.** The client's photo reference is a real child. The photo is kept in
  `references/private/` and is **never** to go on the public board. Needs a game-style character plus a crown. See Q1.
- **The Vaslui Angels** (Princess Pitzi, Ducesa Doina, Lady Lola): in the lore, but no 3D reference yet.
- **The geese.** The client's ref is a 3D goose in a leather aviator cap and goggles. We need three, sized to be ridden.
- **The pig army.** Pigs in oversized medieval armour (client ref), plus Valeriu in armour with the chain on top.
- **The drone shepherd.** A real man from the news. He should be recognisable as him, in the series' 3D caricature style.
- **Night-King Vasile.** Vasile with ice-white skin and glowing blue eyes. He keeps his beanie and beard.
- **The village rapist** from episode 3: only if the gag stays.

**Places and props**
- The Vaslui County map, plus the onion astrolabe.
- The five lands, one rising "clockwork" village each.
- The village square: the existing location `locations/vaslui-square`.
- The Vaslui Iron Throne.
- The drunk army's improvised armour.
- The title card lettering.
