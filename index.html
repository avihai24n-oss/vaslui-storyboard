ESCAPE FROM VASLUI — Episode 02

"THE MIGRANTS"

Status: client's draft, saved verbatim. Changes expected. From Avihai, 29 Aug 2026. This is the source of truth for the episode — the premise text in the folder README is superseded by it. Nothing below has been rewritten or punched up, with two exceptions recorded here:

29 Aug, Avihai: the arrival was changed from the sea/coast to the river Bârlad. Vaslui is landlocked, so a coast could not exist there; the Bârlad genuinely runs through the county. Reference photo: ../assets/reference-river/. Only the two arrival/departure lines were touched — every other beat is exactly as written.
30 Aug, Avihai: the pig's entrance changed. He no longer walks up to the migrants — he arrives by catapult. About two seconds are added ahead of the Mona offer: the tractor-mounted catapult with Valeriu loaded in the tray instead of a stone, the old women firing it, the pig flying, and the landing. Only that one bullet was touched; the Mona offer itself and every other beat are exactly as written. Prop already built: ../../../props/catapult-tractor/.

Immigrants arriving with the boat up the river Bârlad — the real river that runs through Vaslui county, past Muntenii de Jos. Narrow, brown, half-choked with reeds, steep eroded earth banks, broken concrete in the shallows, flat dry farmland either side. Not a sea crossing and not a coast: they have come up a muddy inland river and this is where it runs out.

As they get off the boat and go to land (vaslui village):

IMMIGRANT LEADER (big arab with arab accent, emotional):

"Yalla shabab we finally made it to Europe. We left our homes, our families, paid all the money that we had. All for this moment to start a new life in Europe!"

Then they see everything going on in the village (scene 2). Shots changing between village events and their faces. With each shot back to their faces they look more and more shocked.

Village events:

  • Viorel throwing an axe at an apple on Vasile's head. Both drunk.
  • Villagers drinking, fighting, laying dead drunk in the mud. With drunk animals also.
  • Shot of the Vaslui Strip Club where the strippers are the old village women. One going up and down on a pole, another giving a lap dance, and a third twerking on the bar.
  • The pig arrives by catapult (new, 30 Aug — about 2s): the village catapult, a throwing arm welded onto a clapped-out 1980s tractor, loaded with Valeriu the pig in the tray instead of a stone. The old women of the village are the crew and they fire it. The pig flies across the frame — hat, gold VASLUI chain and a bottle of Mona still in his trotter — and lands on top of one of the migrants, flattening him.
  • Sprawled across the man he has just landed on, the pig holds out the Mona bottle and offers him a drink.

Scene 3

Immigrants looking completely shocked.

ARAB LEADER:

This is what we sacrificed everything for?!

Screams Allaaaaaaaaa (looks at the sky throwing his hands up in the air).

Continues to his deputy standing next to him:

"Ahmad get the boat!"

AHMAD (to leader):

"But Muhammad, we came all this way…."

LEADER (to Ahmad):

"Get the boat Get the boat Get the kusheli ochtak boat" Ya iben sharmuta….

Long curses in Arabic continue while immigrants get back on the boat and go back down the river the way they came.

Scene 4

Shot zooms out to see Mayor Bribeanu in the EU headquarters with politicians watching all of this on a screen.

BRIBEANU (to the politicians, speaking with confidence):

"See, I told you. All you have to do is place some of our Vasluians in your port cities and your immigration problems are over"

EU POLITICIAN (to Bribeanu):

"Mayor Bribeanu — you're a genius!"

(Other EU politicians in the room nodding their heads in approval.)

Wheelbarrows of cash come in the room and are dumped on the table for Bribeanu to take.

He does an evil laugh while the money is being dumped on the table.

The script

7 scenes · one minute · no dialogue

From Avihai, 9 Sep 2026. He flagged it may still shift. Music is the opening's own register crossed with manele.

1 · Clouds

Blue sky, white clouds part. The camera pushes through. THE VASLUIANS appears.

2 · Into the village

The camera dives through the lettering and drops into Vaslui — dirt road, pantile roofs, leaning power poles, sagging wires.

3 · Valeriu — the Bart beat

The church; a wedding inside. Outside the wedding car waits with ribbons and Valerica already in the back. Valeriu finishes his last line in the mud — I WILL NOT DRINK THE MONA — drops the hoof, picks the bottle up and drinks. Jumps in and drives off with her. The bottle stays with him for the rest of the film.

4 · Vasile and Viorel — the Homer beat

A tractor outside the distillery with a concrete mixer hitched behind it; Viorel is already in the drum with a bottle, waiting. Inside: vats, copper pipework, crates to the ceiling. End of shift — Vasile lights a cigarette in the doorway, inside an alcohol plant. He walks out; behind him the whole distillery goes up in blue flame. He does not turn round. Neither of them notices the fire.

5 · Vasilica — the Lisa beat

In the street, the village creep closes in on a young woman. Vasilica, on the bench at the village entrance, draws a slipper and throws it. It hits him square; he lands in a wheelbarrow. She collects her slipper, takes the handles and sets off with him in it.

6 · The convergence

Tractor, wheelbarrow and wedding car all reach the last corner at the same moment and nearly collide. Throughout: a column of smoke standing over the village.

7 · The sofa

A big brown puddle in the middle of the village. An old torn sofa standing in the water, an old television on a crate facing it, its cable running down into the puddle. They all sit. Vasilica tips the wheelbarrow and the creep rolls out face down. The television sparks, smokes, and dies. Black.

What is still missing. No location exists in this look — locations/ holds the village in the series' painted style only. The cast were converted one at a time; the backgrounds have not been started, and they will need a style reference of their own. That is the biggest open item in the episode.

Also outstanding: the witch has no name and no turnaround, Vasilica is drawn too tall against her 3D original, and scenes 4–7 have no shots cut. Scene 5 as written — a man reaching for a woman — will trip content filters; staging the slipper already in flight keeps the joke and clears the filter.

ESCAPE FROM VASLUI — Pilot, Cold Open

"THE BAN"

From Nir's draft, formatted and punched up. Changes are additive — every original beat is intact. Runtime target: 3:30–4:00. Ends on the drop of the Escape from Vaslui song.

FADE IN:

INT./EXT. VASLUI VILLAGE — MORNING (ALLEGEDLY)

Wide. Crooked houses, a dirt road that has never met asphalt. Bottles. Everywhere, bottles — in the mud, on the fences, in a baby carriage.

Villagers young and old lie where they landed. A CHICKEN staggers past a GOOSE. Both are drunk.

VALERIU the pig lies flat on his back, belly to the sky, tongue out, bottles arranged around him like a shrine.

(Hold. Let it be quiet. Somewhere, a cow falls over.)

A broken wooden carriage pulled by a horse that has clearly given up rattles into frame. Standing in it: MAYOR BRIBEANU, 1880s suit, top hat, sash reading MAYOR.

MAYOR BRIBEANU
Fellow Vasluians! Kindly follow me and gather in the village square for an important
announcement from our church community leader.

Villagers look at each other, puzzled.

VASILE (not moving)
We have a square?

EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE — CONTINUOUS

A wooden stage held together by belief. On it: MAYOR BRIBEANU and CARDINAL KIDBANGER, in full crimson-and-white regalia and a tall mitre. He is sweating through it.

The crowd: worn-out villagers, every one holding a bottle. Animals mixed in among them. VASILE and VIOREL at the front. Viorel's axe is on his shoulder, as always.

MAYOR BRIBEANU
Honourable Vasluians. The Cardinal himself has come to Vaslui to bring you a message
from our highest religious order. Cardinal Kidbanger —

(A woman in the crowd grabs her child and hides him behind her back.)

MAYOR BRIBEANU (cont'd)
— you have the Vasluians' ears.
CARDINAL KIDBANGER
Thank you, Mayor Bribeanu.

Bribeanu coughs. Pointedly.

CARDINAL KIDBANGER
Ah. Yes. Of course.

He produces a thick stack of cash and hands it over. Bribeanu pockets it without breaking eye contact with his constituents.

Two villagers exchange a look.

VILLAGER #1
Maybe electing a man named Bribeanu has not been our wisest decision.
VILLAGER #2
He ran unopposed.
VILLAGER #1
He ran unopposed twice.

The Cardinal unfolds a paper. His voice is already shaking.

CARDINAL KIDBANGER
My fellow Vasluians. It has been decreed that from this day forward, all forms of alcohol
will be banned throughout Vaslui.

(Silence. Absolute. A bottle slips out of someone's hand and does not break, because it lands on another bottle.)

Shots of stunned faces. Somewhere in the back, a goose faints.

CARDINAL KIDBANGER (faster, sweating harder)
But it's really not so bad if you think about it! We can go back to drinking water. Reading
books. Developing hobbies. Even showering more regularly. So it's not so bad. Right?
... Right?

A hand goes up.

VILLAGER #3
What if we drink it only for sanitation?

(He holds up a bottle. The MONA label is clearly visible.)

CARDINAL
No.
VILLAGER #4
What if we mix it with water? Then it's mostly water, right?
CARDINAL
No.
VILLAGER #5
What if we also use it as fuel for our carriages?
CARDINAL
No.
VILLAGER #6
What if we also share it with our children?

The Cardinal pauses. Something flickers.

CARDINAL
Hmmm. Children, you say —
MAYOR BRIBEANU (sharp)
KIDBANGER.
CARDINAL (snapping back, shaken)
No. No. Still no.

VALERIU the pig stands up in the middle of the crowd.

VALERIU
What if only the pigs still drink it?

(Beat. Nobody in the crowd finds this remarkable.)

The Cardinal looks out at a village where a pig has just asked a follow-up question, and something in him quietly dies.

CARDINAL
... Okay. No more questions.

He starts to fold the paper. Stops. Remembers.

CARDINAL (cont'd)
Oh — and one last tiny thing. Your village bar will now be turned into a library.

PUSH IN on the crowd. Watch the faces travel, one at a time, from disbelief to rage.

VIOREL takes the axe off his shoulder.

CARDINAL (turning to the Mayor, relieved)
Well. I think that went alright. They seem to be taking it rather well.

HARD CUT.

EXT. THE BIG CHURCH — NIGHT

An OLD VILLAGE WOMAN — VASILICA — marches at the head of the mob holding a spike. On the spike: the Cardinal's mitre. (We do not need to see more than the mitre. It is funnier.)

Behind her, the entire village. Pitchforks. Shovels. Torches. Wheelbarrows at full speed.

They hit the church like weather. Bottles fly. Axes fly. Priests run and are, in passing, beaten. Somebody has brought the pig. The pig is helping.

DISSOLVE TO:

EXT. THE ASHES — LATER

Smoke. Embers. A charred cross leaning at an angle it will keep forever.

VASILE and VIOREL stand in front of it, looking at the ruin. Calm. Unhurried.

VASILE
It's a shame it had to come to that.
VIOREL
Yeah. But what can you do. They really didn't give us much choice.
VASILE
Yeah. I guess not.

They each open a bottle. They clink.

Vasile takes a long drink and looks at the smouldering church with something almost like affection.

VASILE
You know what the worst part is? Now we have to build a new one.
(beat)
Where else are we going to hide the țuică?

SMASH TO BLACK.
